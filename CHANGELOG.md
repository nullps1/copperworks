# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release for Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.

- Nine copper equipment pieces: sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, and a full armor set.
- Four oxidation stages with matching item icons and worn armor textures.
- Natural oxidation of held and worn equipment, accelerated by wet conditions.
- Honeycomb waxing to preserve a stage and axe scraping to remove wax or oxidation.
- Stage-based durability wear, mining speed, attack damage, and armor defense effects.
- Stage, performance, and wax information in equipment tooltips.
- Operator commands for inspecting equipment, setting oxidation, waxing/unwaxing,
  and viewing active server settings.
- Server-controlled balance settings synchronized to connecting clients, with
  independent toggles for each penalty system. Config changes require a restart.
- Age of Copper, It Builds Character, and Not Getting Any Older advancements.
- Vanilla crafting, repair, enchantment integration, and dedicated-server support.
